# Desk notes for the research reports

Optional. To add your own comment to a report, write a line with "## " and the date the report comes out
(the Daily: that morning; the Weekly: the Saturday), then your text below it. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
The report shows it in a "Desk notes" box on the first page, signed with your name. Days without a note publish as usual.

Example (keep the dates in YYYY-MM-DD form):

    ## 2026-10-05
    Semis are leading again and breadth keeps improving. I'm watching the names near a pivot.
