How the market direction is worked out

Market direction is worked out automatically from price and volume and is not IBD's call. Distribution day: the index falls 0.2% or more on higher volume than the day before; it counts for 25 sessions or until the index rises 5% above that close. A rally attempt starts with the first up day after a low; a follow-through day comes on day 4 or later with a gain of at least 1.25% on higher volume. Under pressure: 5 or more distribution days, or the index under its 50-day line. Correction: 8% off the peak, or 6 distribution days under the 50-day line. McClellan oscillator: 19-day minus 39-day average of ratio-adjusted net advances. Not investment advice.

Who reports this week

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Ticker&Tape?

A research platform for stock traders. O'Neil-style charts, RS Ratings, screeners, a heatmap, market breadth, trade ideas and an earnings calendar, all in one place.

Is it free?

Yes. Ticker&Tape is free during the beta. All you need is an email and a password. No credit card.

Which stocks are covered?

More than 2,500 U.S. stocks and ADRs: every member of the S&P 1500 and the Nasdaq-100, plus every other U.S. stock and ADR worth $1 billion or more. ETFs cover indexes, sectors, industries, commodities, bonds and countries.

How often is the data updated?

Ratings, bases and charts are rebuilt after every close, and prices update throughout the trading session.

How is the RS Rating calculated?

It measures a stock's 12-month price performance, with the most recent quarter weighted double, and ranks it from 1 to 99 against every stock we cover. The full formula, and the ones for every other rating, are in the Methodology. New to the terms? See the Glossary.

Can I set alerts?

Yes. Price levels, trendlines and moving averages on any chart, delivered by email and on the site.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Your watchlists, drawings and settings sync across desktop, laptop and phone.

Is this investment advice?

No. Ticker&Tape is a charting and research tool. Ratings and setups are calculated automatically and are a starting point for your own analysis.

contacto@tickerandtape.com, or on X at @Tickerandtape.
