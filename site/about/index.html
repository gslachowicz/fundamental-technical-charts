About Ticker&Tape

Charts, ratings and market breadth for growth-stock traders

Ticker&Tape is a free stock research site built around the way William O'Neil taught people to read the market: buy leading stocks with strong earnings and relative strength, as they break out of sound bases, when the general market is in an uptrend. Everything on the site serves that process.

What you will find

  • Charts in the style of the old printed chart books: daily and weekly bars, RS line, moving averages, labeled volume, the quarterly earnings strip and the current base with its pivot and buy zone. You can draw lines and notes and set price alerts.
  • Ratings for every stock: RS Rating, Composite Rating, SMR Rating and industry group rank, calculated every trading day. How each one is calculated.
  • Screener and watchlists covering every U.S. stock and ADR worth $1 billion or more, plus sector, industry, factor, bond, commodity and country ETFs.
  • Market tools: market direction and breadth, an industry group ranking, a heatmap, comparative charts, an earnings calendar and daily trade ideas.
  • The Ticker&Tape weekly, a free newsletter every Friday after the close.

Who makes it

Ticker&Tape is an independent project made in Buenos Aires, Argentina, by a trader who wanted the charts and screens he uses every day in one place, free and fast. It is not affiliated with Investor's Business Daily, William O'Neil + Co. or MarketSurge; ratings that share a name with theirs are our own approximations, explained on the methodology page.

Data and updates

Prices come from Yahoo Finance: daily bars updated every trading day after the U.S. close, and intraday prices delayed about 15 minutes during the session. Quarterly and annual financials come from Yahoo Finance and from company filings at the SEC (EDGAR). The earnings calendar uses Nasdaq's public calendar. Data can contain errors and delays; check an official source before trading.

Free, with an optional account

The whole site is free. A free account saves your watchlists, chart settings, drawings, notes and alerts across devices. We do not show ads and do not sell your data; see the privacy policy.

Not investment advice. Ticker&Tape is a charting and research tool. Ratings, bases, pivots and trade ideas are calculated automatically and can be wrong. Do your own research and manage your risk.
