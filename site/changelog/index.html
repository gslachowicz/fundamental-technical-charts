What's new

New features and improvements, newest first. Follow @Tickerandtape to hear about them first.

October 4, 2026

Build your portfolio in a minute

Don't remember what you paid? Pick the ticker and the date and use the day's average price, the open or the close with one click, or type the amount you invested and Ticker&Tape calculates the shares. Quick build adds many positions at once: one line per ticker, with an optional date and amount. Estimated prices are marked ≈ in the journal.

Portfolio and trade journal

The new Portfolio section keeps your trades and turns them into positions with live P&L, your results for the day, week, month, quarter and year, and a time-weighted chart against SPY, QQQ, IWM, RSP or DIA. The journal records the setup, stop and a note for every trade, and the stats show your win rate, average gain and loss, profit factor, days held, max drawdown and results by setup. Import the CSV from your broker, export your trades any time, hide the amounts with one click, and get an alert when a position falls to its stop. Private to your account.

Macro calendar and Fed odds

The new Macro section lists the economic releases and Fed events that move the market, in New York time, with the latest official values. On top, the odds of a cut, hold or hike at every FOMC meeting, calculated from fed funds futures. The Daily Tape now opens with the day's calendar and The Weekly Tape adds next week's calendar and the Fed odds.

Research: The Daily Tape and The Weekly Tape

A new Research section with short PDF reports. The Daily Tape comes out every trading morning before the open with overnight movers, earnings, yesterday's breakouts and the leaders near a buy point. The Weekly Tape closes every week on Saturday with prices, breadth, leading groups, earnings and the trade ideas scorecard.

Your own indicators

The new Indicators button on every chart lets you set any moving average (SMA or EMA, any length, color and width), add Bollinger Bands, Keltner Channels and an anchored VWAP, and open up to three panels under the price: RSI, MACD, ADR %, ATR and relative volume. You can also hide the data box or the quarterly EPS and sales table, so every chart looks the way you like. Save your setups as templates and switch with one click. The RS line can now be compared with the Nasdaq-100 or the stock's sector ETF.

Drawing tools

Draw trendlines, horizontal levels and boxes in eight colors, three widths and three line styles, with an optional arrow or a line extended to the right edge. Click any drawing to restyle or delete it. Price alerts now work on trendlines too: the alert follows the line as it moves every session.

Comparative charts, upgraded

Each chart can have its own moving averages and volume (the ⚙ next to each ticker). Eleven new presets cover semis leadership, small vs large, offense vs defense, credit, copper vs gold, inflation, cyclicals, momentum, international markets and crypto. In the Performance % view, click any day to start every line at zero from there.

Keyboard shortcuts

On a chart: T trendline, H horizontal line, R box, N note, Delete removes the selected drawing, Ctrl+Z undoes, D and W switch daily and weekly, I opens indicators, A sets an alert, and the arrow keys move through your list.

October 2, 2026

Comparative charts

Stack several tickers on one timeline, like SPY, RSP and MAGS, or chart ratios such as RSP:SPY. Pick your own moving averages, switch to a performance view, and share any comparison with a link. Open comparative charts.

Cleaner links and new pages

Every chart and section now has its own address, like tickerandtape.com/chart/NVDA/, that you can bookmark and share. New About and Methodology pages explain how every rating is calculated.

October 2026

Market breadth

Market direction the O'Neil way, with distribution and follow-through days, plus stocks above their moving averages, new highs and lows, the advance/decline line and the McClellan oscillator. Open market breadth.

Trade ideas, earnings calendar and the weekly newsletter

A daily scan for leaders near a buy point, an earnings calendar with each stock's setup, and the Ticker&Tape weekly every Friday after the close.

Heatmap, sectors and commodities

An S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 heatmap that can be colored by RS Rating, and dashboards for sector ETFs and commodity futures on the home page.

Industry groups, chart wall and alerts

Group rankings by relative strength, a grid view of every chart in a list, and alerts on price, trendlines and moving averages, by email and on the site.
