Glossary

The terms you will find across Ticker&Tape, in plain words

Charts, ratings and setups come with their own vocabulary. Here is what each term means on Ticker&Tape. For the exact formulas behind every rating, see the Methodology.

ABCDEFHIMNPRSU
A
A/D lineBreadth
The running total of advancing minus declining stocks, day after day. A rising line means most stocks are joining the move, not just the biggest names.
AntsChart
Dots on the chart when a stock closed up in at least 12 of the last 15 sessions on volume at least 20% above its 50-day average. They turn gold when the stock also gained 20% or more in those 15 days: a sign of steady institutional buying.
B
BaseSetups
A period of sideways or corrective trading, at least 5 weeks long, measured from the stock's highest high of roughly the last 65 weeks. Most big moves start from a sound base.
BreakoutSetups
A close above the pivot, ideally on heavy volume. On Ticker&Tape the status stays Breakout while the stock is no more than 5% above the pivot.
Buy pointSetups
See Pivot.
Buy zoneSetups
From the pivot up to 5% above it: the normal range to buy a breakout. Past that, the stock is Extended.
C
Composite RatingRatings
One number from 1 to 99 that blends EPS growth (counted double), the RS Rating, the industry group rank, the SMR Rating and the distance from the 52-week high.
CorrectionMarket
The market's weakest phase: the index is 8% off its peak, or has piled up distribution days while below its 50-day line. Most breakouts fail here.
CupSetups
A base 15% to 50% deep with a rounded bottom, without a handle, trading within 10% of its high.
Cup with handleSetups
A cup followed by a short pullback of 2% to 15% in the upper half of the base, lasting up to six weeks. The pivot is the high of the handle.
D
Deep correctionSetups
A pullback of more than 50% from the high. Not a buyable base.
Distribution dayMarket
The index falls 0.2% or more on higher volume than the day before: a sign that large investors are selling. It counts for 25 sessions, or until the index rises 5% above that close.
E
EPS growthFundamentals
Earnings per share in the latest quarter compared with the same quarter a year earlier. Leaders usually show strong and accelerating growth.
ExtendedSetups
More than 5% above the pivot: past the normal buy range, where the risk of a pullback is higher.
F
Failed breakoutSetups
A stock that broke out and then closed more than 3% below the pivot.
Flat baseSetups
A tight base no more than 15% deep, often forming after a prior advance.
Follow-through dayMarket
On day 4 or later of a rally attempt, a gain of at least 1.25% on higher volume. It confirms a new uptrend.
H
HandleSetups
The short, shallow pullback on the right side of a cup that shakes out weak holders before a breakout.
I
Industry group rankRatings
Every GICS sub-industry ranked by the average relative strength of its stocks. Rank 1 is the strongest group. Most big winners come from the top groups.
M
Market directionMarket
The current phase of the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq, read from price and volume: Uptrend, Uptrend under pressure, Rally attempt or Correction.
McClellan oscillatorBreadth
The 19-day minus the 39-day average of ratio-adjusted net advances. Above zero, breadth is improving; below zero, it is weakening.
Moving averagesChart
The 21-day exponential and the 50- and 200-day lines on every chart. Leaders tend to stay above their 50-day line and find support near it.
N
New highs and lowsBreadth
How many stocks hit a new 52-week high or low on the day. More highs than lows is a healthy market.
P
PivotSetups
The buy point of a base: the high of the handle, or of the left side when there is no handle, plus $0.10.
R
Rally attemptMarket
Begins with the first up day after a market low. It needs a follow-through day to turn into a confirmed uptrend.
RS lineRatings
The stock's price divided by the S&P 500. A rising line means the stock is beating the market; a new high in the RS line ahead of the price is a sign of leadership.
RS RatingRatings
Relative strength from 1 to 99: the stock's 12-month price performance, with the latest quarter weighted double, ranked against every stock covered. 80 and up is leadership territory.
S
SMR RatingRatings
A grade from A to E for Sales growth, profit Margins and Return on equity.
StopSetups
The price at which to cut a loss. Trade ideas show a stop 7% below the pivot, the classic maximum loss on a new position.
U
Up/down volumeChart
Volume on up days divided by volume on down days over the last 50 sessions. Above 1 points to accumulation; below 1, to distribution.
Uptrend under pressureMarket
The market is still in an uptrend but shows 5 or more distribution days, or the index is below its 50-day line. A time to be selective.
